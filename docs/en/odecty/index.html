Odečty

Energy use and bill estimates.

Beta on TestFlight

Odečty records your electricity, gas, water and heating meter readings and charts your consumption. Based on your contract it estimates whether your next bill will bring a refund or an extra payment.

Key features

  • Read meters by taking a photo
  • Consumption charts and year-on-year comparison
  • Bill estimate and suggested advance payment
  • Alerts for unusual consumption
  • Reminders for readings and fixed-price end

Privacy Policy
