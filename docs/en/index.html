How the apps work together

Every app works on its own – connections are a bonus. Have several and they lend each other a hand: one opens the other exactly where you need it, while your data stays on your phone.

  • →Save a place from Jídelník to Navigace and get directions there.
  • →At the end of your route, Jídelník finds a good place to eat.
  • →Save your vet from Mazlíček as a place in Navigace and drive there.
  • →Store a service or tyre receipt from Garážka with its warranty in Záruky.
  • →Split trip costs from Cestovníček with friends in Vyrovnáno.
  • And EasyForLife Hub shows what’s coming up from all apps in one place.

How we build them

Accounts only where they make sense

Small apps work right away with no sign-up. Only services that sync with the web or serve businesses need an account.

No ads, no tracking

The apps contain no ads or ad tracking and never sell what you do in them.

Your data is yours

Small apps keep data on your phone or in your private iCloud. Services with accounts never sell your data or share it for advertising.

Independent, yet connected

Every app works on its own. Use several from one family and they work hand in hand.
