Cestovníček

Day-by-day trip plans with transport, stays and AI.

Coming sooncestovnicek.luksafoto.cz ↗

Cestovníček plans your trip day by day: transport, accommodation, places to see and budget. AI can suggest a whole trip or fill in a single day.

Unlike the other EasyLife apps it uses an account, so your plans sync between iPhone, iPad, Mac and the web.

Key features

  • Day-by-day itinerary with budget
  • AI plans for a whole trip or a single day
  • Route map and tips for places nearby
  • Quick links to Booking, Airbnb, flights and trains
  • iPhone, iPad, Mac and web
