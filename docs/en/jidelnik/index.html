Jídelník

The best restaurants nearby or anywhere.

Beta on TestFlight

Jídelník finds the best restaurants nearby or anywhere by address and ranks them by a smartly weighted rating. It helps you decide where to go and remembers the places you want to try.

Key features

  • Search by cuisine, radius, opening hours and price
  • Service filters: delivery, terrace, dogs, accessibility
  • Map and details with photos and reviews
  • Favourites, Want to try and Visited lists
  • “Pick for me” dice, reservations and widgets

Works with

SalónkyAt the airport it shows your flight and the lounges you can use.
