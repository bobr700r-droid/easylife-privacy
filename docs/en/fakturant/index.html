Fakturant

Invoices, expenses from receipts and finances for freelancers.

Open web appAccount

WebiPhone

Fakturant is simple invoicing for freelancers and small businesses: invoices, advance invoices, credit notes, numbering series and QR payments.

It also tracks expenses, bank payments and taxes. Runs on the web and iPhone, with your own account.

Key features

  • Invoices, advance and recurring invoices, credit notes
  • PDF with QR payment, email and reminders
  • Customers from the Czech business register, EUR invoices
  • Expenses from receipt photos, bank and taxes
  • Multiple companies and user permissions
