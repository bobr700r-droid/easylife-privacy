FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. EasyLife apps work without sign-up or login. The only exception is Cestovníček, which uses an account to sync with the web.

Where is my data?

On your iPhone and, if the app syncs, in your private iCloud. We have no access to it. Each app’s privacy policy has the details.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Apps that sync via iCloud move your data automatically when you sign in with the same Apple ID. Everything else comes over with your iPhone backup.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes the data on your device. Data in iCloud can be deleted in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

How do I join a beta?

Some apps are still in beta testing on TestFlight. Send us an email and we’ll invite you.

Great! Send us an email – we read every suggestion.
