Salónky

Lounge cards, boarding passes and flight alerts.

Beta on TestFlight

Salónky keeps track of your airport lounge cards and how many visits you have left. It reads your flight from a boarding pass and tells you when the gate changes, the flight is delayed or it has landed.

Key features

  • Lounge cards with remaining visits
  • A log of your lounge visits
  • Flights read from boarding passes (photo or PDF)
  • Alerts for gate changes, delays and arrival
  • Live flight status on the Lock Screen and a widget

Works with

JídelníkFinds a good place to eat at the airport or at your destination.

Privacy Policy

The app is in beta testing on TestFlight.
