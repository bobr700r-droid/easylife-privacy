Záruky a doklady

Receipts, warranties, documents and inspections in one place.

Coming soon

Záruky a doklady keeps receipts, warranties, personal documents and inspections in one place, and reminds you before a warranty or document expires.

Key features

  • Scan receipts and extract the details
  • Expiry tracking for ID, passport and licence
  • Warranty claims with deadline tracking
  • Reminders for boiler, chimney and other checks
  • Document numbers locked behind Face ID

Works with

GarážkaTracks your car’s inspections, insurance and service.
PředplatnéKeeps an eye on recurring payments and subscriptions.
